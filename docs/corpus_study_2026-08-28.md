## Verified findings (the load-bearing set)

**The grammar (35/35, zero violations).**
`chain := [phase_c [spring_test]] [mini] (sos [last_supper])* [mini] lps` → buy.
The LPS is terminal 35/35; **trigger price = the terminal LPS's final-bar high to the
penny, crossed exactly one bar later, 35/35** — the mark-set is built backwards from the
buy, and in 20/35 the buy sits at or below R. The first third of every box is silent
(earliest event start 0.37 of the span). Event windows relay and nest (29/53 consecutive
pairs gap ≤ 1 bar; EGBN's LPS contains its own SOS) — disjoint-episode parsers mis-read a
third of the corpus.

**SOS (22 drawn).** A short violent traverse: span 1–9 bars (median 3.5), travel
0.89–2.40 box-heights (median 1.68), ~1 ADR/bar, efficiency ~1.0. Against 47 clean
unmarked rallies in the same boxes, the separation is **net speed (5.5×) and one-way-ness
(4.5×) — never magnitude, never volume** (7/22 below-average volume; the boxes' loudest
bars are all unmarked failures). 22/22 launch from the lower half, 9/22 from below S (the
spring welded in); 3/22 never close above R — arrival is graded, never gated
(ruling 2026-08-26 confirmed). The keep is definitional: no marked SOS ever closes below
its launch low within 6 bars. Boundary trio MAIN +17.0%/26d, RRC +14.8%/20d,
XRN +10.9%/16d: all refused on span+speed while their magnitudes sit inside the accepted
range — speed carries the decision. **Verifier caveat (load-bearing):** the fitted
8-clause bar accepts 22/22 drawn and 0/47 clean negatives, but an exhaustive ≤9-bar
window scan finds ~20 distinct unmarked waves that pass it — the bar measures
decisiveness; it is NOT a classifier alone. Election and story context are load-bearing,
and three lens-fitted threshold sets on two ADR baselines await unification + the
operator's eyeball.

**Last Supper (10 drawn).** The first full giveback swing after an SOS in every drawn
specimen — opens on the extension top (highest print bar one, lowest bar last, 10/10),
2–5 red-dominated bars, **1.68–3.19 ADR deep (median 2.06)**, retracing 45–88% of the SOS
advance (median 70%), landing at the old rail (all 10 LPS lows then form within 3.2% of
R), no volume signature (0.64–1.42×). **Not-a-warning holds in data:** all 10 preceded
+17% to +134% forward, median entry never underwater; the deepest giveback preceded +43%.
**No bonus either:** forward median 22.6% vs 29.6% for the fair no-LS control —
descriptive, zero points. Typed-event verdict: GO-shaped (tight envelope on six
dimensions, detectable inside the R-rail wave machinery as the SOS wave's reaction leg),
build reserved for the operator. **Ruling 2026-08-28 supersedes the sample:** the anchor
is ANY preceding up-move (SOS / push off an LPS / breakout) — the 10/10-after-SOS
statistic is sample fact, not definition.

**LPS (36 drawn).** Three invariants at 36/36: span 2–5 bars; pullback depth **0.9–2.7 ×
ADR** (raw % spans 2.2–12.7 — ADR is the eye's ruler, never percent); and the quiet-final
union (final bar quiet in volume OR range) at 35/36 — while the engine's
`lps_vol_contraction` gate fails 9/35 of the operator's own winners (dry-up is tendency,
not rule). Placement follows cause: ceiling rest 30/36 (the on/above-R shelf is the most
common terminal form and the least modeled — `lps_pullback_profile` fails exactly the 5
above-R shelf winners); valley 6/36, every strict one licensed by a drawn spring. The
snap-back form is real but rare (4/36); EGBN itself is a holding shelf.

**Spring (10) / test (5) / band (5).** The drawn spring is the penetration leg only
(10/10 end below S — compare tips, never windows); depth 0.9–3.5 ADR; elected by
consequence, never depth; 5/10 open at/above R (upthrust-into-shakeout as one swept

the ATR scale stays coherent. The spring test: 1–3 bars after the spring, higher low above
the tip at/below S, 5/5 tip-anchored (quietness is a 4/5 tendency, graded not gated). The
band: 0.8–1.9 ADR tall, 12–42 bars, three roles (press-under-lid / base-on-base above R /
post-shakeout rebuild), the launchpad of the terminal move 5/5, soft containment (ROIV's
band contains its own SOS and Last Supper); the engine's inner-box search already
converges on it (DSGN fired the drawn band as its own box).
