# Anchor-bar study — the operator's eye, measured (2026-07-25)

Instrument: `tools/anchor_bar_study.py` (read-only; sealed fixture +
calibration DB; fingerprint `b671e056…`, engine `aaf853bd…`). Population: all
33 Guided List marks — every one carries declared `r_anchor_date` /
`s_anchor_date` and `first_rail`.

## Findings

1. **Rails are exact bar extremes — fidelity 0.000 on all 66 rails.** Every
   drawn R equals its anchor bar's High and every drawn S its anchor bar's
   Low, to the cent. The rails-at-H/L ruling is now measured fact.
2. **The box is born at its first swing.** The R anchor is the drawn
   window's FIRST bar (median position 0.000) and resistance is drawn first
   28/33; the S anchor follows within the opening bars (median position
   0.030). Climax high → first reaction low: the operator's box begins
   where the engine's climax→AR model says a box begins.
3. **Drawn rails tolerate heavy later overshoot.** The window's extreme
   sits a median **1.6 ATR above the drawn R** (0.99 ATR below the drawn S);
   a median ~12 bars' highs exceed his R inside his own box. He does not
   redraw for excursions — the level is proven instead by re-touch
   (median 11 touches of R, 15 of S, after the anchor bar).
4. **Anchor bars are positional, not special.** Slightly wider than the
   window median (×1.2–1.35), unremarkable volume (×1.0–1.1); R-anchor bars
   close in their lower third (rejection character), 31/33 are local pivots.
   Nothing about the bar's own size/volume identifies it — its ROLE (first
   swing of the base) does.
5. **The engine already stands on his bars.** The elected (hits) or
   nearest-to-drawn (misses) candidate anchors on his EXACT bar 27/64 rails,
   median 2 bars apart — and on the misses, median **0 bars apart**. Rail
   placement is not the eye-gap; the gap is that the gates then reject the
   framing those bars propose, because of the overshoot his eye tolerates
   (finding 3) and dwell judgments (the margin campaign).

## The bounded-compensation hypothesis — tested and FALSIFIED

The proposed gate-topology change ("a near-miss on one leg rescued by large
surplus on others") was tested on the margin instrument's rows before any
engine work. Result: **junk fails narrow-but-rich exactly like the marks.**
EGBN's profile (lower-dwell −1 bar; surplus +12: up +8, mid +2, respect +2)
has exact junk twins — DGII (low −1, up +9, mid +4, out +1: surplus 14),
BMRN (low −1, up +8, mid +2, out +4: surplus 14), GOOD (low −1, surplus 49) —
15 junk candidates fail exactly one leg by exactly one bar. No surplus
threshold, and no leg-specific compensation rule, separates EGBN from DGII
on these statistics. **Tested-DEAD: do not re-propose compensation over the
worked-equilibrium/respect margin legs.** Whatever distinguishes a
high-holding shelf (EGBN) from descent-tail dead space (DGII) for the eye,
it is not present in this margin algebra — finding it means measuring
something the engine does not yet measure, not re-weighing what it does.

## Consequence

The eye-gap frontier after three programs (gap-breach, rail, this study):
anchors aligned, floors at the junk boundary, compensation dead. The
remaining misses are separable only by NEW measured information (the cause
and direction of a dwell deficit; the story context of an excursion), and
any such signal enters measure-first per the standing doctrine.
