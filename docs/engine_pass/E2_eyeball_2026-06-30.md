## How to read the surface
`python -m tools.l2_staircase_audit TITN` (or no args for latest-scan fires) now prints, under each
staircase, a `narrative:` line + the `trace`:

```
narrative: chronology=<intact|partial|absent> completeness=k/4 [+terminal-upthrust]
           spine[spring=<date|-> sos=<date|-> lps=<date|->]  tests=N
  | C[bar ..]: spring - breach S, reclaimed in N bars (undercut x ATR)
  | D[bar ..]: SOS - break above R held near R (hold x box)
  | D[bar ..]: LPS - support test held (<swing_type>)
  | -> chronology partial, completeness k/4
```

- **spine** = the best-of-class canonical pieces: the spring, the **first** confirmed SOS (the break above R),
  the LPS. `-` = absent (reported, never fabricated).
- **chronology** = `intact` only when spring **and** SOS **and** LPS are all present and bar-ordered
  (spring tip < SOS peak < LPS low). Most real fires are `partial` (a spring-path **or** an SOS-path, rarely
  both) — that's expected; it is a richness descriptor, **not** a pass/fail.
- **completeness k/4** = how many of {spring, SOS, LPS, ≥1 held test} are present. A descriptive tally.
- **+terminal-upthrust** = a run-up that topped in an upthrust with **zero SOS** and nothing after it
  resolving up or still developing (the TITN read). Suppressed honestly while the right edge is unresolved.

## Two decisions for your eyeball (surfaced, not auto-decided)
1. **`markup`-after-upthrust.** The build treats a `markup` reach **after** the last upthrust as
   *un-terminal-ing* it (price advanced and held above R ⇒ not a terminal failure). Alternative reading: leave
   it terminal because no break-above-R **SOS** was confirmed. Current choice is the conservative one (fewer
   terminal flags). Your call on whether that matches how you'd read it.
2. **Phase-C spring weight in the 0..4 tally.** E2 counts a Phase-C spring equally with the Phase-D pieces
   (raw descriptive count). E3 is where weighting happens — confirm you want E2 to stay an unweighted tally.
