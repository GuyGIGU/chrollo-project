the numbers turn out to favour.

## Population and outcome

Every matured screener episode (US equities) in a read-only copy of the archive taken Tue 13/10/2026. The new
reader reads each episode's archived days in order, on the cached daily bars up to each day, until it fires (the
method of 12/10/2026). Outcome: the best gain within **40 trading days** of the fire day's close. The tail first:
the share reaching **+25%** and **+40%**; the median best gain beside them.

## The six measures (fixed now)

On **the setup's box** (the box the LPS was read against: the mini when the LPS was read against the mini, else the
parent). **dr** = that box's daily range frozen at its birth. **The base** = the box's opening day to the fire day.

| # | Measure | Plain words | "Tight / clean" end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | height | (R − S) / dr — how many daily ranges tall the box is | small |
| 2 | quiet bars | median daily bar (High − Low) in the base / the same over the 60 trading days before the box opened | small |
| 3 | dead space | the grade's empty days / base days (bars that miss the box) | small |
| 4 | wrap | (\|R − mean of the base's 3 highest highs\| + \|mean of its 3 lowest lows − S\|) / (R − S) — how far the lines sit from where the bars actually turn | small |
| 5 | tightening | mean daily bar of the last 10 trading days / mean daily bar of the whole base | small |
| 6 | LPS depth | (highest high from the day before the LPS to its last day − lowest low of the LPS days) / dr | small |

Reported beside them, never judged: the box height in % of price (expected to follow the stock's volatility).

## The read

Quarters cut **inside each scan month** (tight to loose), because the months ran very differently. Per quarter:
P(+25%), P(+40%), median best gain. Tight quarter minus loose quarter on P(+25%) with a 95% interval that
resamples scan DAYS. The same quarters inside each ADR third and inside each month.

## The bar

A measure **HOLDS** only when all three are true:

- (a) the tight quarter beats the loose quarter on P(+25%) with the interval above zero;
- (b) the same direction in at least 2 of the 3 volatility thirds;
- (c) the same direction in most scan months.

Six measures are tested at once, so one that only just clears (a) is reported as a **lead**, not a finding.
"Holds" means: worth building as a fact the screen shows and the archive keeps (measure-first). Never a gate, never
points in the grade without his word.

## Amendment, before the full run (Tue 13/10/2026, 20:10)

A smoke test on 12 tickers (28 episodes) printed the facts of 8 fires; no table, no quarter, no comparison was
computed. It showed two definition faults, fixed here before the run:

1. **The base ran into the right side.** Ending it on the fire day put the push over resistance inside it (NOV, Fri
   07/08/2026: wrap 4.09 — the "highest highs" were the run after the breakout, not the base's tops). The base now
   ends **the day before THE SOS's push**, else on the fire day: the grade's own base (its rule 2). Tightening keeps
   its words: the last 10 trading days up to the fire day against that base.
2. **Two boxes mixed.** Dead space is the grade's, and the grade reads the PARENT whatever box the LPS was read
   against (its rule 1); the other five on the mini would mix two boxes. **Every fact is read on the parent.** Which
   box the LPS was read against, and whether a mini stands, are reported beside them (never judged).

3. **The wrap divided by the box made every tight box a bad wrap.** NOV's parent is 1.1 daily ranges tall, so one
   poke of half a range reads as half a box (wrap 4.09 even on the amended base). The wrap is now **in daily
   ranges**, and on the 90th / 10th percentile of the base's highs / lows (robust to the one or two named pokes his
   rulings call evidence, never a fault): (|R − 90th pct of highs| + |10th pct of lows − S|) / dr.
